Reading the numbers

Data notes — Sandwiches

  • Labneh and Tuna — no weight or slice count recorded (Labneh, Tuna)
    The field team recorded length but no weight or slice count for these two items, so their value comparison is per cm only. No per-100g figure is estimated.
  • Turkey & Cheese — measured by slice count (Turkey & Cheese)
    This item was recorded by number of slices rather than weight, so a per-slice figure is shown alongside per cm. It appears as a separate marker in the dashboard and is never substituted for the main metric.
  • Joe & The Juice Chicken Caesar — split weight combined (Chicken Caesar)
    The workbook lists 40 g (chicken) and 12 g (turkey) for this sandwich. The two are added to 52 g for the per-100g figure.
  • Where per-100g is (and isn't) available (Halloumi, Chicken Caesar, Rosto)
    Weights were recorded only for Halloumi, Chicken Caesar and Rosto. For those items the dashboard's portion-adjusted view uses per-100g; for every other item it uses per cm.
